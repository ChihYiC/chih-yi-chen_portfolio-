# Overview

This project examines how AI-generated children’s books are disclosed and presented on online marketplaces.

As AI-assisted publishing becomes more common, disclosure practices can vary across product titles, descriptions, author information, and metadata. This project explores how visible, consistent, and understandable these disclosures are for users browsing children’s books online.

## Focus

The project focuses on:

* AI disclosure visibility
* Product description practices
* Online marketplace transparency
* User trust and interpretation
* Children’s book publishing and AI-generated content

## Research Area

This work connects human-AI interaction, platform transparency, and digital marketplace design. It considers how users encounter AI-related information and how disclosure design may shape trust, evaluation, and decision-making.
