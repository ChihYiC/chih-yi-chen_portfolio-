# Project Overview

Breakfast Online Ordering is a mobile app redesign project for a breakfast restaurant. The goal was to improve the ordering experience by making menu browsing, item customization, quantity editing, checkout, and membership features easier to use.

The project focused on reducing ordering friction for customers who want a fast, clear, and convenient breakfast ordering experience.

***

## Problem

The original app had low user engagement and received poor feedback from customers. Through user interviews and observation, I identified several usability issues:

* Menu categories were difficult to navigate.
* The app lacked a search function.
* Users had trouble modifying item quantities after submitting an order.
* Membership benefits were unclear.
* Some users found the menu font too small and difficult to read.

These issues made the ordering process slower and less intuitive, especially for users who wanted to quickly place a meal order.

## Goal

The goal of this redesign was to create a smoother and more user-friendly mobile ordering experience by improving:

1. Menu organization
2. Search and navigation
3. Quantity modification
4. Checkout flow
5. Membership visibility
6. Overall customer engagement

## My Role

UX Researcher & UI Designer

I worked on user interviews, problem identification, empathy mapping, user journey mapping, flowchart design, wireframing, visual design, and Figma prototyping.

## Research Process

I conducted user interviews with four customers between the ages of 20 and 55. The interviews focused on understanding how users browse the menu, customize their orders, complete payment, and respond to membership features.

Based on the findings, I created an empathy map and user journey map to identify pain points across the ordering experience, from discovery and menu selection to checkout and post-order feedback.

### Key Insights

Users wanted a faster and more convenient way to find meals, customize items, and complete orders.

The main pain points were unclear menu categories, the lack of a search function, difficulty modifying quantities, and unclear membership benefits. These issues reduced users’ motivation to use the online ordering app.

### Design Solution

The redesign focused on simplifying the app structure and improving ordering efficiency.

Key design improvements included:

* Clearer menu categories
* More visible item information
* Improved ordering and checkout flow
* Easier quantity adjustment
* Membership-related functions
* A brighter visual style using blue and yellow to reflect energy, morning, and breakfast freshness
* Final Outcome

The final Figma prototype presents a cleaner mobile ordering flow that supports browsing, customization, cart management, payment, and order confirmation. The redesign aims to help customers complete breakfast orders with less confusion and fewer steps.

## Reflection

This project helped me understand how small usability problems can strongly affect customer engagement. I learned the importance of connecting interview findings with interface decisions, especially for everyday service apps where users expect speed, clarity, and convenience.
